This page summarizes the products and services we deliver for NGINX on Azure and points you to the full 'Products & Services' catalogue. Below is a concise list of the major offerings and how each can help your business. For the complete, detailed master list (product pages, pricing, and deployment options), see: /nginxaas/azure/products-and-services/

- NGINX App Delivery — High-performance reverse proxy and load balancing that improves application availability and response times.
- NGINX Ingress Controller for Kubernetes — Native Kubernetes ingress to manage traffic routing, SSL termination, and microservices exposure at scale.
- Managed Load Balancing & Autoscaling — Managed services that simplify capacity planning and ensure applications scale reliably under load.
- Performance Tuning & Caching — Optimization and caching strategies to reduce latency and lower infrastructure costs.
- Security & Web Application Firewall (WAF) — DDoS protection, bot mitigation, and WAF rules to protect applications and data.
- Observability & Logging — Monitoring, tracing, and centralized logging to accelerate troubleshooting and improve reliability.
- DevOps Automation & CI/CD Integration — Pipeline and configuration automation to speed releases and enforce consistency.
- Professional Services & Consulting — Architecture design, migrations, and custom integrations to align solutions with business goals.
- Managed Support & SLAs — 24/7 operational support and clearly defined SLAs to reduce downtime and risk.
